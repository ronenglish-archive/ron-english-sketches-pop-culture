South Park Last Supper Panel B

Original Drawing

  • Title: South Park Last Supper Panel B
  • Medium: Photocopy collage on paper
  • Dimensions: 14 × 17 in.
  • Work ID: RE-014971-B
  • Signature: Signed “ENGLISH,” lower right
  • Provenance: Artist's Personal Collection

Notes

Panels A and B constitute a single work presented on two separate sheets of paper, each measuring 14 × 17 in.

The composition consists of photocopies of the original figure drawings, cut out and pasted onto the sheets to assemble the South Park Last Supper scene. See the other panel.
